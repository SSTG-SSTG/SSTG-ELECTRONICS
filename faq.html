Frequently asked questions

Cannot find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

What do you repair?

We repair TVs (LED, LCD, Smart), ACs, washing machines, refrigerators, laptops and mobile phones.

Do you come to my home?

Yes. Doorstep service is our main service. A technician visits your home.

Do you offer same-day service?

Yes, same-day service is available. Contact us early in the day and we will confirm a visit time.

How do I book a service?

Fill the booking form on the Book service page. It opens WhatsApp with your details filled in. You can also call +91 82207 32684.

How much will it cost?

Our charges are affordable. Tell us the device and the problem, and we will share the charges.

Do you repair all brands?

Washing machine repair covers all brands. For other devices, tell us the brand and model and we will confirm.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Coimbatore. Send us your area and we will confirm that we can visit.

Can you install my AC?

Yes. We do AC installation and uninstallation, gas filling, cooling fixes and regular maintenance.

Should I back up my laptop or mobile data?

Yes. Save your important files before any repair, as a precaution.
