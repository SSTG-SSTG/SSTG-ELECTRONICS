How doorstep repair works

Three simple steps from the problem to the fix.

  1. Tell us the problem

    Use the booking form, call +91 82207 32684, or WhatsApp us. Mention the device and what is wrong.

  2. We confirm a visit

    We reply with a visit time for your area. Same-day service is available.

  3. Technician at your door

    The technician checks the device at your place and repairs it.

Before the technician arrives

A few small things make the visit faster.

Keep the device accessible

Clear space around the TV, AC, washing machine or fridge.

Note what happens

When did the problem start, and is there any sound, light or error?

Keep model details handy

The brand and model number help us prepare for the repair.

Back up laptop and mobile data

Save your important files before any software or hardware work.
